BENCH BOOST - HOW TO UPLOAD THIS SITE TO GITHUB PAGES
=====================================================
This zip is the WHOLE WEBSITE and nothing else.

1. Unzip it on a computer (Windows: right-click > Extract All).
2. On github.com open your repository > Add file > Upload files.
3. Open the unzipped folder, press Ctrl+A to select everything INSIDE it, and drag it all into the browser window.
   (Drag the contents, not the outer folder, so index.html sits at the top level.)
4. Wait until every file is listed, then click "Commit changes". The new index.html replaces the old one.
5. Wait 1-2 minutes, open your site, and press Ctrl+Shift+R.

You can leave out this HOW-TO-UPLOAD.txt file; the site does not use it.
Full instructions and a 5-minute check: docs/DEPLOYMENT.md (in the FULL project zip).
Do NOT upload anything from the LEGACY folder - that is the old app.
